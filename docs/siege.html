SIEGE // TERRAN SHELTER

The zerg horde hits your Command Center in timed sieges. Between sieges: mine minerals (edge fields tick automatically), build walls, turrets, bunkers, depots, refineries and a lab, and run research. During a siege: packs pour through edge gates that roll arithmetic ops (+ − × ÷) changing their fighter count — click to shoot them yourself, and shoot the ☢ barrels for scrap to forge bigger guns. Survive as many sieges as you can!
