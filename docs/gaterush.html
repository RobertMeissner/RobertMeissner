GATE RUSH // TERRAN

Zerg packs pour through the four edge gates — each gate rolls an arithmetic op (+ − × ÷) that changes the pack's fighter count. Build walls, turrets and cannons around your Command Center, shoot barrels for gold (or let your guns pop them), tap packs to shoot them yourself, and upgrade your guns. Survive the waves!
