MARINE RUN // FRONT LINE

A 3D lane runner. Your squad charges forward and shoots non-stop — swarm packs storm in from far away down one of 3 lanes. Steer with the mouse or A/D / arrow keys. Each stretch has a lane of enemies, a lane of +5 recruits to shoot for more fighters, and a lane with a GUN+ crate for weapon upgrades. Gate walls span the whole road — you always pass through one of 2–3 gates: +N / ×N grow your count, −N / ÷N shrink it, solid walls cost 12. Bigger stack wins a clash. A behemoth boss waits at the end of every level. Count hits 0 = over!
