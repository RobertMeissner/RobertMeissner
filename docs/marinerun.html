MARINE RUN // TERRAN

Side-scroller in the style of Last Human: Final Stand's runner combat. Your marine squad runs and shoots non-stop. Steer with the mouse (or W/S/arrows) through pairs of math gates — +N and ×N grow your fighter count, −N and ÷N shrink it. Blast zerg packs (bigger stack wins on contact), grab +N crates, dodge spikes and spitters, and take down the Ultralisk boss at the end of each level. If your count hits 0, it's over!

FIELD MANUAL

mouse / W-S / arrow keys: steer up & down (the run is automatic, guns always firing)

green gates +N ×N = grow your number · red gates −N ÷N = shrink it

zerg packs: your number vs theirs — bigger stack wins the clash, loser is wiped

+N crates add fighters · spikes and hydralisk/turret spines chip your count · boss at each level's end

P: pause · desktop recommended
